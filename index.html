Input format

lat,lng,weight,label
40.811,-73.946,3,cafe
40.728,-73.990,1,park

lat and lng are required. weight and label are optional. Pasted points can skip the header — bare lat,lng pairs work too. Rows with bad coordinates are skipped.

everything runs in your browser; nothing leaves the page.
